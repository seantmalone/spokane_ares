Summary

The screens are, on the whole, well designed for the people they were built for. They give one form per fact and plain words, the server refuses what it must, and every save says where the change shows. Net Settings, Meeting Schedule and the page-text lock are the strongest. The problems sit around the screens rather than in them.

The headline: live content doesn't go through those screens. Net control, meetings and events are entered by the webmaster over SSH (O1). That skips every check and leaves the schedule's Undo able to wipe the edits (N3). The single biggest improvement is a process change: AG7QP and the EC enter what they already write, on accounts made for them. After that come the gaps at the ends of each lifecycle. Postponed events nobody revisits, documents whose Undo can't bring the file back, a restore nobody has rehearsed.

Tuesday net and meetings

Update the Net Control Schedule and Winlink assignments

Sound

ARES Editor · monthly, or when the Dashboard says the schedule runs out in under 3 weeks

The model is right: four plain states per Tuesday, weeks worked out from the date rather than stored, and a clash check on each row, so two editors can't overwrite each other. The weak point is the input. Every value is typed again from a page that already holds it, and refused typing is easy to lose.

Findings

  • N1Medium Every month the schedule is typed again from ag7qp.com or e-mail

    The source of truth is AG7QP's page and mail. The editor copies each call sign and Winlink task by hand. There is no import, and the call-sign datalist only helps with calls already used.

    plugins/spokares-core/inc/admin-rota.php:113-287

    Fixed in 0.3.0Paste the schedule, with a preview, on the Net Control Schedule.
  • N2Medium Four screens, three ways of handling a clash

    The schedule and Cancel or Move use a hash per row. Net Settings and Meeting Schedule use a snapshot per field. Undo and SSH edits have no check at all. Held typing lasts 5 minutes and is read once on the option screens, but lasts 7 days on events and documents.

    inc/admin-rota.php:423-431; inc/admin-net.php:711-723; inc/admin-common.php:206-235; inc/admin-events.php:354

    PartlyHeld typing now behaves the same everywhere (N5) and Undo checks rows (N3); the per-row and per-field clash checks remain two mechanisms.
  • N5Low Refused typing on the option screens lasts 5 minutes and survives one view

    Reload the page after a 'Saved, except…' notice, or wait too long, and the typed text is gone.

    inc/admin-common.php:206-235

    Fixed in 0.3.0Held typing lasts until the next save of that screen, up to 24 hours.
  • N6Low The site shows 5 Tuesdays; the screen takes up to 52

    This is intended, and the save notice already warns when a saved date is beyond the fifth Tuesday. Recorded so nobody 'fixes' it.

    inc/render.php:434-449; inc/admin-rota.php:510-521

    By designNo change needed.

Recommendations and what they change

  • Nexteffort M
    Add a 'Paste the schedule' box: paste lines from ag7qp.com or the e-mail, see a preview of which Tuesdays change, then save.

    In the appA parser and a preview step in admin-rota.php. Its output goes through the existing per-row checks, so call signs, never-publish words and clashes are still checked. No new data.

  • Nexteffort S
    Keep refused typing until the next save of that screen, or for 24 hours, not 5 minutes and one view.

    In the appChange spokares_retain/spokares_retained in admin-common.php to delete on save rather than on read. This also aligns with events and documents (N2).

Undo or redo the last schedule save

Risky

ARES Editor · rare, after a mistaken save

Undo for a whole table is a good idea for nervous volunteers, but as built it reverts by position, not by change. It can't tell a row nobody touched since that save from one someone else fixed in the meantime. SSH edits never write a snapshot, so the danger grows with the current way of working (O1).

Findings

  • N3High Undo puts back the last save with no check and no confirmation

    One click swaps the snapshot rows back in, whoever made that save. Rows changed since then are overwritten without a word. That includes SSH edits, which never write a snapshot, so Undo can quietly wipe them.

    inc/admin-rota.php:551-622

    Fixed in 0.3.0Undo and Redo skip rows changed since, name them, and ask first for someone else's or an older save.

Recommendations and what they change

  • Noweffort S
    Undo only the rows that are still exactly as that save left them. List any it skips ('Oct 20 was changed by NZ2S since; left as is').

    In the appStore each row's hash in spk_rota_prev at save time and compare it before swapping in spokares_handle_undo_rota(). It's the same hash the save already computes.

  • Noweffort S
    Ask before undoing a save made by someone else, or one more than a day old.

    In the appAn inline confirm step on the rota screen (no browser dialog), driven by spk_rota_saved's user and stamp.

Exercises and events

Add or change an event

Needs work

ARES Editor · as needed, about 3 minutes

The shape is good: the type decides which boxes appear, the notice says exactly which list the event lands in, and a published event is never pulled by a typo, because the rest saves and only the bad box waits. That partial save is the most complex behaviour any editor meets, though, and three rough edges undercut it: a real bug with multi-day timed events, a draft notice that hides problems, and a late past-date check.

    inc/admin-events.php:151

  • E2Medium A wrong year is caught after the event is live

    The past-date check runs after publishing, as an amber notice. It also fires on every edit of an old exercise ('has passed… check the year'), which is noise for historic records.

    inc/admin-events.php:640-709

    Fixed in 0.3.0A past date is asked about before publishing, only when the dates changed.
  • E5Low Boxes hidden by the event type keep their old contents

    Change an Exercise to Public service and the tasks, links and button are still stored, but not shown or checked. Switch back and old text, or an old phone-number confirm, reappears.

    inc/admin-events.php:165, 195

    Fixed in 0.3.0Unused boxes are cleared on save, and named.
  • E6Low 'Draft saved' doesn't mention the problems

    Save draft with errors and the notice is core's 'Draft saved'. The red outlines are there, but the message implies all is well.

    inc/admin-events.php:754

    Fixed in 0.3.0'Draft saved. 2 boxes need fixing before you can Publish.'
  • E7Medium 'Change or cancel an event' opens a list, not the event

    The commonest edit is a click on the Dashboard, a scan of the list, and a click on the name. Documents has the same detour.

    inc/dashboard.php:328-350

    Fixed in 0.3.0The Dashboard lists the next five events, each linked to its form.
  • E9Low The 'where it shows' notice copies the theme's block settings

    inc/admin-events.php:557-586

    Fixed in 0.3.0The limits are read from the theme's patterns.

Themes across the workflows

The guided screens are bypassed

The plugin's best work (plain words, guard rails, clash checks, undo, notices that say where a change shows) is aimed at volunteer editors, but live content goes in over SSH (O1). Until AG7QP and the EC use their own accounts, most of that value goes unused and the webmaster is the bottleneck.

One save model, not four

Holding refused input, catching clashes and undoing a save are each done two or three different ways (N2). One shared helper, with a hash or snapshot per item, held typing that lasts until the next save, and an undo that skips changed items, would make every screen behave the same and would remove N3 and N5 as a side effect.

Report what a change knocks over

Meeting Schedule names the one-date changes a pattern change removes (M3). The same courtesy is missing when Winlink weeks move (N4), when a Most Used document goes to draft (D12), and when Pull breaks links (D11). The fix is the same small pattern each time.

Deep links, not lists

The Dashboard's 'Change or cancel an event' and 'Replace or change a document' open lists, and the review count opens a sorted list that isn't filtered (E7, D3). Short lists of the next items, each with a direct link, would save a click and a scan on the commonest jobs.

The documents disagree

ux/journeys.md (v0.1.1) still describes Duplicate, a Trash row action and restore-as-draft for events. The runbook contradicts itself and GitHub on approval (O4). ops/README.md and the runbook describe different release flows. EDITING-GUIDE.md matches the code. Mark journeys.md as historical, and fix the two ops documents.

Reviewed and fine as it is

  • Quick Edit and bulk edit are refused on the server, not just hidden. This is right for a guarded workflow.
  • Restoring an event from the Trash puts it straight back on the site (unlike core, which restores to draft). It matches what 'Undo' means to an editor.
  • Public lists are worked out from today's date at render time, with no cron. Nothing to schedule, nothing to drift.
  • The schedule screen shows 13 rows and posts up to 52, and the site shows 5 (N6).
  • There are only two offered roles, and the Net grant is removed automatically on demotion.
