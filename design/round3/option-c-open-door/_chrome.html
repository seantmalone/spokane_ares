Rules

  • Only the chrome repeats. Nothing else appears on every page: no strips, banners, next-net lines or "this week" cards.
  • The header is not sticky. The members sub-nav sticks from 641px wide; on phones it wraps to two rows and scrolls away. The How it works chip bar and About TOC are page content, not chrome.
  • "Join the team" is the only filled button in the header and the only use of seal red on the site. It always points to index.html#join.
  • The footer states one fact, the safety line "Volunteers, not first responders. In an emergency, call 911." Contact details live on about.html#contact; the footer only links there.
  • No href="#" anywhere. "Privacy (coming)" is plain text until the page exists.
