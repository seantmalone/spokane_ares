Option B, "Carry the Message": canonical chrome (round 3)

Copy these blocks verbatim into every page, in this order: the head block; at the top of <body> the sprite, then the skip link and header; the page's <main id="main"> (members pages: inside the members shell); then the footer and the two script tags. DESIGN.md §0 has the simplification rules and §5 the builder rules. This file is a reference, not a page of the site.

What changed in round 3 (and why)

  • No utility bar. The night strip with the next net, "Copy radio settings" and For Members is gone from every page. Each of those facts has one home: the net on how-it-works.html#nets, the dated rota and the copyable settings line on members/index.html#rota, and For Members in the primary nav.
  • Header: brand, four links, Join the team. It is the only sticky element on any page.
  • Members sub-nav: one light row, five labels, always visible and never sticky. It has no sidebar, group labels, next-net readout or dead # links. "Task books" goes to ../about.html#training-path.
  • Footer: the seal, six links, the safety line and the copyright and mockup line (33 visible words). No address, mission line, "Formerly…", affiliation marks or audience columns.
  • Head script: it also turns smooth scrolling off on a cold load with a hash, so site.js hashLanding() can land deep links exactly.

Active state

  • Primary nav: put aria-current="page" on the current section only: Home, How it works, About ARES & ACS, or For Members (on every members/*.html). CSS draws the amber bar.
  • Members sub-nav: put aria-current="page" on "This week" (members/index.html), "Exercises & events" or "Documents & forms". "Task books" and "groups.io" never carry it.
  • Paths: members pages prefix every link and asset with ../ and set <body data-root="../">.
  • Body classes: page-home, page-how, page-about, members-page.
