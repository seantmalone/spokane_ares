The same three sites, at about a fifth of the words

Each site went from about 18,000 words to about 3,400 (18–21% of round 2). All three now share the same trimmed copy, so they differ in layout and tone, not content. Compare with the round-2 gallery.

Open Channel still leads at 8.35, ahead of Carry the Message (8.24) and Figure One (7.86). The newcomer and editor judges picked Open Channel; the member judge picked Carry the Message. All 18 pages are within their budgets.
