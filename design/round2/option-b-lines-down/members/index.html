    Staying current

    A reference, not a tracker: nothing here is saved.

    Every month

    • Check into a net (weekly encouraged).

    Every quarter

    • Attend a Third Thursday meeting or a Second Saturday workshop (monthly encouraged).

    Every two years

    • First Aid and CPR (RADO and up).
    • SKYWARN spotter training (encouraged).

    Every year

    • Take part in one field operation: an activation, an exercise or a public-service event.
    • Check your personal kit (24 hours minimum, 72 preferred) and your radio kit (2 m and 70 cm for 24 to 72 hours).
    • Program the repeater and simplex channels into your handheld.
    • Radio Operators and up: serve as net control at least once.
    • Senior Radio Operators: send and receive Winlink; lead or assist an exercise, activation or event.

    Always

    • Keep an ICS-309 during Auxiliary Communications: Spokane County Emergency Management’s own volunteer radio program. Members pass a county background check. radio operations, and an ICS-214 during exercises and activations (RADO and up).
    • Keep your ALERT Spokane (opens spokanecounty.gov) registration current. It now runs on Regroup.
