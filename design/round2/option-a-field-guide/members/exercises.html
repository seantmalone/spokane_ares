Oct3Sat

Simulated Emergency Test (SET)

ExerciseNational

The ARRL’s annual national exercise, held on the first weekend of October. The Eastern Washington Section Emergency Coordinator sets this year’s scenario and asks every group to do the tasks below. This year’s scenario is lighthearted, but the skills are the same as for a real deployment.

When
Sat, Oct 3, all day
Where
From your station. Local plans (where to be, who is The operator who runs the net, calling check-ins and keeping order.) are on groups.io.
Bring
A station that can send by Winlink (radio preferred; Telnet is acceptable), and a camera or phone for photos.

Your tasks, from the Section’s 2026 SET request

  1. Email by radio. (radio preferred; Telnet acceptable): send a Washington State Field Situation Report to your EC and to the Section Emergency Coordinator (AG7QP), and send a message through the National Traffic System to the Section Manager (KA7LJQ) and the Section Emergency Coordinator (AG7QP).
  2. Take pictures of your station and operation to share.
  3. Make as many contacts as you can using the rules of POTA and/or SOTA.
  4. Send an after-action report (AAR) with a list of participants to the Section Emergency Coordinator.
  5. Have fun.

ARRL SET forms(opens arrl.org) Section SET instructions(opens ag7qp.com)

Oct15Thu

Great ShakeOut: send a DYFI message

ExerciseNational

At 10:15 AM, send a USGS “Did You Feel It?” (DYFI) report by Winlink. It’s the same report Standing Order 1b asks for after a real earthquake, so this is practice for the day it counts.

When
Thu, Oct 15, 10:15 AM
Where
Wherever you are.
How
Use the DYFI form in Winlink Express. Clearly mark it as an exercise.
Dry run
Two days earlier, Tue, Oct 13, the Winlink-night assignment is also a DYFI report.

Great ShakeOut(opens shakeout.org) USGS Did You Feel It?(opens usgs.gov) Standing Order 1b
