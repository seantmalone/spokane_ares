Staying current

From the ACS Task Book, version 2024-08-09. A reference, not a tracker: nothing here is saved.

Every month
  • Check into a net (weekly encouraged).
Every quarter
  • Attend a Third Thursday meeting or a Second Saturday workshop (monthly encouraged).
Every year
  • Take part in one field operation: an activation, an exercise or a public-service event.
  • Check your personal kit (24 h minimum, 72 h preferred) and your radio kit (2 m / 70 cm for 24–72 h).
  • Program the repeater and simplex channels into your handheld.
  • Radio Operators and up: serve as net control at least once.
  • Senior Radio Operators: send and receive Email by radio.; lead or assist an exercise, activation or event.
Every two years
  • First Aid/CPR (RADO and up).
  • SKYWARN spotter training (encouraged).
Always
  • Keep an ICS-309 during Auxiliary Communications, Spokane County Emergency Management’s volunteer radio program. radio operations; keep an ICS-214 during exercises and activations (RADO and up).
  • Keep your ALERT Spokane registration(opens spokanecounty.gov) current (it now runs on Regroup).

On groups.io

Our groups.io group is the member hub: the e-mail list, calendar, files and wiki. A moderator approves each new member, and the archive, files and wiki are members-only.

  • Files(opens groups.io, members only)The Spokane ACS Field Operating Guide (ACSFOG, half- and full-page), the net roster, exercise plans.
  • Calendar(opens groups.io, members only)Meetings, nets and events.
  • Subgroups(opens groups.io)Digital-Modes, HamWan, Hospital-Coordination, Leadership, SHARES, Winlink, WinterFieldDay. Some are restricted.
  • Not a member yet? Apply to join(opens groups.io)A moderator approves each request.

Never copy ACSFOG content, county channels or the roster onto a public site or social media. Channels and procedures are covered by the county’s non-disclosure agreement, and the roster holds members’ personal details.
