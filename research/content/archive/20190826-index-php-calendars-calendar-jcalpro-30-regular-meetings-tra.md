# Events under 'Regular Meetings/Training'

These are dates for the regular monthly meetings, digital training sessions, and other meetings or training not otherwise needed for deployment qualifications but for expanded capabilities of members.

×

### [ARES Net](https://spokares.org/index.php/calendars/calendar-jcalpro/30-regular-meetings-training/243-ares-net)

##### Tuesday, August 08, 2017 20:00 - 21:00

Every Week until August 07, 2022

Weekly Net operations and training session.  Held on the Spokane County ARES / RACES Repeater at 147.30 MHz, with positive 600 KHz offset and a 100Hz tone for access.

×

### [Second Saturday Amateur Workshop](https://spokares.org/index.php/calendars/calendar-jcalpro/30-regular-meetings-training/1-digital-training)

##### Saturday, August 12, 2017 9:00 - 12:00

Every Month until July 12, 2022

Monthly training opportunity to learn about various Amateur radio communication methods, build stuff, test stuff, learn things.  Primarily focused on emergency communications and preparedness.  The Topics vary each month from digital comms to emergency power and building go-kits.
