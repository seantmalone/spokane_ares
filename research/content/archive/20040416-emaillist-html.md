The ARES/RACES Email List

The ARES/RACES E-Mail list is a list of the ARES/RACES members in the Spokane County ARES/RACES group that have internet access and would like to be notified of activities regarding the activities of the Spokane County ARES/RACES group. This would include Weather related events along with Public Service events and local disaster call outs. It could also include ARRL related information and other Ham related Internet sites.

The E-Mail list is being kept by me and will not be distributed for any reason. At this point in time I have not asked your permission to distribute the list.

If you would like to be placed on the ARES/RACES E-Mail list please send E-Mail to KD7AMV requesting that you want to be put on the list. If at any time you wish to be removed from the list please do the same.
