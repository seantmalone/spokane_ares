**Alert Resources**

- **GENERAL**

  - [Alerts | Washington State Military Department, Citizens Serving Citizens with Pride & Tradition](https://mil.wa.gov/alerts)

- **TSUNAMI:**

  - Tsunami alerts: <https://mil.wa.gov/alerts#tsunami>
  - Tsunami sirens: <https://mil.wa.gov/tsunami#sirens>
  - Tsunami app for WA and OR: <https://www.nanoos.org/mobile/tsunami_evac_app.php>
- **VOLCANO:**

  - Volcano Notification Service: <Http://volcanoes.usgs.gov/vns>
- **EARTHQUAKE:**

  - ShakeAlert: Earthquake Notification App (Not EQ prediction)
  - [www.cisn.org](http://www.cisn.org) : real-time world-wide earthquake alert and notification system
  - ShakeMap: graphical tool to portray extent of shaking following an earthquake

    - <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/shakemap>
  - Did You Feel It?: Citizen science for shaking intensity, created by public input

    - <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/dyfi>
  - ShakeCast: Post-Earthquake situational awareness app

    - Estimates shaking at your facility (bldgs., roads, bridges)
  - ENS (Earthquake Notification System):

    - <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/ens>
  - [PAGER](http://PAGER): rapid estimate of earthquake impact using Hazus info
  - Aftershock Forecast Map: estimates the number of aftershock and probability of subsequent larger earthquakes

    - <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/oaf>

Any questions? Feel free to reach out to [public.education@mil.wa.gov](mailto:public.education@mil.wa.gov)
