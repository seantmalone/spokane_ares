### **CERT** Go Kit

### Your **CERT** Go kit should be a readily available kit of essential supplies and equipment. The following is only a suggested list - you should customize your go kit for your needs and the situations you may face.

### Basic Kit

Backpack\
Hard hat\
Eye protection\
**CERT** vest\
Work leather gloves\
Flashlight/Extra Batteries\
Dust masks (4)\
Ear plugs (2 sets)\
One quart water\
Hand sanitizer\
1st aid kit\
Whistle\
Leatherman type tool\
Duct Tape & Plastic Tarp\
Knife\
Water pump pliers or gas shut-off wrench\
Local Maps

### First Aid Kit

Sturdy weather-proof container\
Disposable surgical gloves (10)\
CPR Mask with one-way valve\
Safety glasses/Face Shield\
Small Sterile pads (4"x4") (10)\
Large Sterile pads (5"x9") (2)\
Large Sterile pads (8"x10") (1)\
2" roller gauze (2)\
4" roller gauze (2)\
Tape (1" width)\
Antibiotic Ointment\
Sealable quart-sized bags (2)\
Triangular bandages (2)\
Splinting material\
Safety Shears\
Splinter Tweezers\
Alcohol-based hand sanitizer\
Bio-hazard waste bag (2)

### Other Considerations

**Your personal needs:**\
Personal Medications\
Food\
Water\
Personal hygiene items\
Emergency Information Card\
- Your Full Name\
- Emergency contact Name & phone number\
- Medical Information\
-- Medical Conditions\
-- Medicine taken\
-- Allergies\
-- Blood Type\
-- Religion\
-- Special instructions

FRS/GMRS/Amateur Radio (if licensed)
