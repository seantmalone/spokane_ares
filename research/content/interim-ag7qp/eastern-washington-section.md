### Eastern Washington (EWA) Section

Covering the 20 counties of Eastern Washington

**Section Manager**: Jo Whitney, KA7LJQ, [redacted: contact detail]\
**Section Emergency Coordinator**: Frank Hutchison, AG7QP, [redacted: contact detail]

The EWA Section has three nets **held each week on Monday**:\
- A VHF Net held at 1815 (early check ins start at 1800) Pacific Time on the [KBARA repeater network](https://kbara.org/) and linked repeaters.\
- A Winlink Net with Winlink Check In Reports submitted to EWA-WL2K between 1700 and 1900.\
- A HF Net held at 1845 (early check ins start by 1840) Pacific Time on 3985 KHz with secondary frequencies of 3990 and 3995 KHz. Net control may shift up or down frequency because of interference or prior occupancy as necessary.
