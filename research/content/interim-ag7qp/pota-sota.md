### POTA and SOTA Locations

Jeff Banke, NZ2S, has provided the following list of POTA sites in the Spokane area:\
Literally every Riverside Park location, every Centennial Trail Trailhead, every State Fish & Wildlife fishing lake is a POTA site. So let's start with the Lakes where you can activate only from the boat launch area or from on the water:\
Amber Lake | Badger Lake | Deer Lake\
Diamond Lake | Eloika Lake | Farragut State Park (Idaho)\
Farragut Wildlife area (Idaho) | Fishtrap Lake | Jumpoff Joe Lake\
Hayden Lake (Idaho) | Hog Canyon Lake | Horseshoe Lake\
Liberty Lake | Newman Lake | Silver Lake\
Sprague Lake | Waitt's Lake | Williams Lake\
***Then there are the other sites like:***\
Blackwell Recreational Area (Idaho) | Blue Creek (Idaho) | Centennial Trail (Multiple access points)\
Columbia Plateau (Multiple access points) | Cougar Bay (Idaho) | Dishman Hills\
Dragoon Park | Ice Age Flood Zone Trail (Multiple access points)\
International Selkirk Loop (Multiple access points) | Mt. Spokane State Park | Priest Lake State Forest\
Priest Lake State Park (2 access points, Indian Creek & Lions Head) | Riverside Park (Multiple access points from Hayward in Liberty Lake to 9 Mile Dam)\
Rustler's Gulch | Steptoe Battlefield | Steptoe Butte | Turnbull Wildlife area
