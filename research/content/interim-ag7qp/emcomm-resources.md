### NAWN Training Videos

(Almost) Every Month on the third Wednesday, Elder Dave Carleton, K7DSR/WRCY281, holds a training session for Emergency Response Communicators (ERC) located in the United States West North (USWN) Area of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. These sessions are usually recorded. A list of all the recordings is located [here](https://sites.google.com/view/emerpreperc/home).

### [I Am Safe Messaging System](https://radiorelay.org/iamsafe/)

The I Am Safe messaging system was developed for **the transmission of welfare related messages from a disaster area**, as a cooperative effort between the Seattle Emergency Communication Hubs (<http://seattleemergencyhubs.org/>), Seattle Auxiliary Communication Service (ACS) (<https://www.seattleacs.org/>), Radio Relay International ([radiorelay.org](http://radiorelay.org)) and the Winlink Development Team ([winlink.org](http://winlink.org)). The Northwest Washington American Red Cross provided additional input with regard to the development of short message texts.

All materials for the I Am Safe messaging system are available from [Radio Relay International (RRI)](https://radiorelay.org/iamsafe/)
