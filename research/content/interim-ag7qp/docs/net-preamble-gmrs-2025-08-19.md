Spokane County

Auxiliary Communications System

ACS GMRS Net Preamble/Script

Please read the following announcement on the air at about 1925 hours:

Attention all stations on frequency.  This is Net Control Station {Your GMRS Call Sign}. The Spokane County Auxiliary Communication System GMRS Net will start in about five minutes.  At that time all stations will be invited to check in as directed by net control.

At 1930 hours:

Attention all stations on frequency. This is Net Control Station {Your GMRS Call Sign} calling the Spokane County Auxiliary Communication System GMRS Net. This is a directed net, occurring the third Tuesday of each month at 1930 hours local time on the WRCY281 GMRS repeater.

The regular Spokane County weekly Amateur Radio net is held every Tuesday night at 2000.  In months with a fifth Tuesday, the net is held on the Spokane County ARES-ACS simplex frequency in place of our regular net on the Spokane County ACS repeater.

{Unkey; Pause only momentarily}

The purpose of this net is to provide information and coordination concerning the joint activities of Spokane County ARES-ACS group. It is also intended to serve as a monthly emergency net training session for those services, to allow participants to test their equipment, verify improvements, and check propagation.

All stations stand by for check-in. Be advised that STATIONS WITH EMERGENCY OR PRIORITY TRAFFIC MAY BREAK IN AT ANY TIME.

{Unkey; Pause only momentarily}

Please observe proper procedure for all transmissions. Visiting stations will be invited to check-in after the call for Spokane County ACS members. Please state your call sign slowly and phonetically.

{Unkey; pause, let the repeater drop out}

Use the separate ARES Roster for the following

This is Net Control Station {Your GMRS Call Sign}. Standby for Spokane County ACS member check-in. Check-in will be ordered in groups by call sign suffix. When checking in, list all traffic then hold for direction from Net Control. Any station needing to leave the net immediately after checking in, please advise Net Control and wait to be excused. Please remember to state your call sign slowly and phonetically.
