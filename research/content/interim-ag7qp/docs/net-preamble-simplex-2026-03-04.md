Spokane County

ARES-ACS Simplex Net Preamble

Amateur Radio Emergency Service/Auxiliary Communications System

Note to Net Control: It is important on simplex to repeat back each call sign slowly, phonetically and completely as they check into the net.  This is for those stations that others do not hear. That way the whole net knows whose playing and can compare how they are doing across the whole area from net to net or as they improve their antenna system or propagation improves or declines.

At the appointed time:

Attention all stations on frequency. This is Net Control Station {INSERT CALL SIGN HERE} calling the Spokane County ARES-ACS Net. This is a directed net on this simplex frequency held every fifth Tuesday of the month at 2000 hours local time.

This net normally meets every Tuesday at 2000 hours locale time on the Spokane County ACS Repeater.

All stations are invited to check in.  Also, because this is simplex, we will be requesting a readback of the number of stations heard from everyone that checks in.  Therefore, please have paper and pencil ready to record the call signs plus the signal strength of each station you hear.  There is a roster on the Spokane County ARES website to aid you in recording this information.  In addition, you are requested to send your list of stations heard to [redacted: contact detail], who will compile a comprehensive contact report that will be available on our website.

{Unkey; Pause only momentarily}

The purpose of this net is to provide information and coordination concerning the joint activities of Spokane County’s ARES-ACS group. It is also intended to serve as a weekly emergency net operations training session.

Please note that announcements and traffic will be made after this simplex net on the regular repeater frequency.  So, at the conclusion of this net, please switch to the Spokane County ACS Repeater.

{Unkey; Pause only momentarily}

All stations standby for check-in. Be advised that STATIONS WITH EMERGENCY TRAFFIC MAY BREAK IN AT ANY TIME.

{Unkey; Pause only momentarily}

All other stations, please observe proper procedure for all transmissions. Visiting stations will be invited to check-in after the call for Spokane County ARES-ACS members. Please state your call sign slowly and phonetically.

{Unkey; pause}

{Establish one or two net control relays if needed}

Net control will periodically call for relays.  Please do not volunteer relays until net control has called for them.  Also, when operating as a relay, please use call signs so that others who only hear one side of transmissions will understand who is being called. All others please be patient during relays; you might only be hearing one side of the conversation.
