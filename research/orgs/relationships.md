# Spokane County ARES/RACES — Related Organizations
> Researched 2026-09-25. Light pass: relationships and links from discovery evidence; not every fact independently re-verified. See _backlog.md to go deeper on any org.

**How to read this file**

- **Tiers.** Core means the new site must describe or link the org correctly. Related means it deserves a mention or a link. Peripheral means link targets only.
- **Confidence.** *Confirmed* means a discovery agent saw it on the cited page. *Likely* means it came from a search snippet, a bot-gated page or indirect evidence. *Speculative* means it is our own reasoning. Text marked INFERENCE is reasoning, not a statement from a source.
- **Dates.** "Seen" dates are 2026-09-25 unless stated.
- **Scope of this pass.** No new web checks were run for this wrap-up. Contradictions are listed below, not resolved.
- **Change risk.** Names, callsigns, frequencies and meeting times change often. Treat every one of them as "verify before publishing".

    - The Welcome Letter signs W7TSC as "Spokane County Emergency Coordinator and RACES Officer" ([Welcome Letter](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/contact/welcome-letter)).
  - **Meetings** are at SCEM, 1121 W Gardner Ave. The current schedule on the temporary page is: 1st Tuesday 5:30 PM staff meeting, 2nd Saturday 9 AM workshop, 3rd Thursday 6 PM training, 4th Tuesday 6 PM new-member orientation ([ag7qp.com Spokane page](https://ag7qp.com/spokane-county-ares-acs), [orientation](https://ag7qp.com/new-members-orientation)).

### 3. ARRL national and ARRL Northwestern Division (ARRL; the League; ARRL Emergency Communications & Field Services; Board EC-FSC committee; Northwestern News; SEA-PAC)

- **What:** ARRL is the national amateur radio association and the sponsor of ARES. The Northwestern Division is the ARRL region of six sections that includes Eastern Washington.
- **Relationship:** The ARRL sits at the top of the ARES authority chain and holds the national served-agency agreements the group relies on.
  - **Affiliation.** The About page states the ARRL affiliation ([About](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/4-about-spokane-county-ares-races)). The Spokane Plan calls ARES an ARRL activity run through Field Services ([Plan](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/2-uncategorised/2-ares-races-plan)).
  - **Officers and staff** (seen 2026-09-25):
    - President Rick Roderick K5UR and CEO David Minster NA2AA ([officers](https://www.arrl.org/officers)).
    - Emergency Communications-Field Services Director Josh Johnston KE5MHV ([contact](https://www.arrl.org/contact-arrl)).
    - Field Organization supervisor Steve Ewald WV1X ([field org](https://www.arrl.org/field-organization)).
    - EC-FSC chair Scott Yonally N8SY ([committees](https://www.arrl.org/board-committees)).
  - **National agreements** Spokane relies on ([served agencies](https://www.arrl.org/served-agencies-and-partners)):
    - Red Cross MOU, renewed April 2021.
    - NWS SKYWARN, 1986.
    - FEMA MOA, 2023.
    - Salvation Army/SATERN.
    - NVOAD membership.
    - RRI MOU, 2025-07-18 ([news](https://www.arrl.org/news/radio-relay-international-and-arrl-sign-memorandum-of-understanding)).
  - **Northwestern Division:**
    - Director Mark Tharp KB7HDX and Vice Director Michael Sterba KG7HQ have served terms from January 2025 ([election results](https://www.arrl.org/news/2024-arrl-board-of-directors-election-results), [sections](https://arrlnwdiv.org/sections/)).

    - SEA-PAC is next held June 4-6, 2027 ([ag7qp.com](https://ag7qp.com/)).
  - **Policy tension.** The July 2025 ARES Plan requires full ARRL membership for Intermediate level and above and for EC/AEC appointments. The Spokane Plan says membership is not required to join ([ARES Plan](https://www.arrl.org/files/file/ARES%20Plan%20July%202025.pdf)).
- **Links:** https://www.arrl.org/ · https://arrlnwdiv.org/ · https://www.arrl.org/served-agencies-and-partners
- **Confidence:** Confirmed ([officers](https://www.arrl.org/officers), [served agencies](https://www.arrl.org/served-agencies-and-partners), [NW Division sections](https://arrlnwdiv.org/sections/)).

### 4. ARRL ARES program (ARES Plan July 2025; ARES Task Book July 2024; ARES levels; ares.arrl.org Forms 1-4 and FSD-98; ARRL Learning Center Basic, Intermediate and Advanced EmComm; ARES Leadership course; SET; Field Day; PSHR; ARES E-Letter; ARES Connect, retired)

- **What:** The ARRL program, standards, training and reporting that the "ARES" half of the group follows.
- **Relationship:** The group's program membership and its reporting line.
  - **Levels under the July 2025 ARES Plan** ([ARES Plan](https://www.arrl.org/files/file/ARES%20Plan%20July%202025.pdf)):
    - Basic: FEMA IS-100, 200, 700 and 800, ARRL Basic EmComm and the Level 1 Task Book.
    - Intermediate: adds Intermediate EmComm and full ARRL membership.
    - Advanced: adds IS-230, 240, 241, 242, 244 and 288. Advanced is required for EC, AEC, DEC and SEC, and leaders also take the ARES Leadership course.
  - **Mismatches on the current site:**
    - The Join page requires the Task Book but links only the generic arrl.org/ares page ([Join](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/contact/join-ares-races)).

    - The ag7qp.com guide does follow the 2025 Plan ([ARES Task Book guide](https://ag7qp.com/ares-task-book)).
  - **Reporting.** The EC reports monthly on Form 2 and the SEC on Form 4, both at ares.arrl.org. FSD-98 is the registration form ([forms](https://www.arrl.org/public-service-field-services-forms), [ares.arrl.org](https://ares.arrl.org/)). The Net Manager's statistics flow from the EC to the SEC to the League ([Leadership](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/about/ares-races-leadership)).
  - **Courses.** In May 2024, EC-001 and EC-016 were replaced by Basic and Intermediate EmComm. Advanced EmComm is "on hold" ([news](https://www.arrl.org/news/arrl-learning-center-features-two-new-emergency-communication-training-courses)). ARRL credits Jack Tiley AD7FO "of Spokane County ARES/RACES" with the 2011 EC-001 materials ([instructor resources](http://www.arrl.org/emergency-communications-field-instructor-resources)).
  - **SET 2026** runs Oct 3-4 ([SET](https://www.arrl.org/simulated-emergency-test)) and is on the Spokane calendar for Oct 3 ([Spokane page](https://ag7qp.com/spokane-county-ares-acs)). The IEVHFRA MOU names SET as joint training.

- **Links:** https://www.arrl.org/ares · https://www.arrl.org/files/file/ARES%20Plan%20July%202025.pdf · https://www.arrl.org/files/file/ARES%20Taskbook%20July%202024.pdf · https://ares.arrl.org/ · https://www.arrl.org/emergency-communications-training

### 24. Legal and regulatory framework (FCC; FCC ULS; 47 CFR Part 97 Subpart E; 47 CFR 97.407; RCW 38.52 (.010, .070, .180, .310); WAC 118-04, amended effective 2025-12-27; EMD-024/025/078 forms; WA Labor & Industries; usraces.org, non-governmental)

- **What:** The federal and state rules that license operators and define volunteer status and protections.
- **Relationship:** These rules regulate all operations.
  - **FCC.** The FCC licenses every member and W7GBU.
    - The Plan cites Part 97 Subpart E as its first authority and operates under ARES rules unless a national emergency requires RACES-only operation ([Plan](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/2-uncategorised/2-ares-races-plan)).
    - 47 CFR 97.407 (last amended 2010) requires civil-defense certification for RACES stations and operators, limits RACES traffic, and caps drills at 1 hour a week, or 72 hours twice a year with approval ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-E/section-97.407)).
  - **RCW 38.52** ([RCW 38.52](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=38.52), [.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=38.52.010)):
    - .010 defines an emergency worker as someone registered with a local EM organization who holds its ID card.
    - .070 authorizes local and joint EM organizations; this is the basis of SCEM's ILA.
    - .180 covers liability; the county application cites it for vehicle use.
    - .310 covers registration and coverage.
  - **WAC 118-04** ([WAC 118-04](https://app.leg.wa.gov/Wac/default.aspx?cite=118-04&full=true)):
    - Registration is a "prerequisite for eligibility ... for benefits and legal protection".
    - Workers register on EMD-024 or an equivalent; the county application is that equivalent.
    - There is a "Communications" class of emergency worker, and training missions are covered.
    - The chapter was amended effective 2025-12-27.
  - **L&I.** The county Volunteer Policies page references L&I coverage ([policies](https://www.spokanecounty.gov/1824/Volunteer-Policies-Procedures)). The Welcome Letter's link to the L&I bloodborne-pathogens course is dead.
  - **usraces.org** is a private organization, not an authority ([Ham Radio Links](https://www.spokares.org/index.php/membership/ham-radio-links)).
- **Links:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-E/section-97.407 · https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=38.52 · https://app.leg.wa.gov/Wac/default.aspx?cite=118-04&full=true
- **Confidence:** Confirmed ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97/subpart-E/section-97.407), [RCW 38.52](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=38.52), [WAC 118-04](https://app.leg.wa.gov/Wac/default.aspx?cite=118-04&full=true)).
